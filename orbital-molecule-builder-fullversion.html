How it works
1Pick an element, then tap empty space in the viewport to place an atom.
2Switch to Bond mode and tap two atoms in a row to connect them — choose single, double, or triple above.
3Use Move mode and drag an atom to reposition it; bonds follow automatically.
4Drag on empty space to orbit the camera. Pinch to zoom, drag with two fingers to pan. (Mouse: right-drag orbits, scroll zooms, middle-drag pans.)
5Toggle Orbitals for a simplified, illustrative view of bonding and lone-pair electron density.
